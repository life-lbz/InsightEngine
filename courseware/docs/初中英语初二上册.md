# 初中英语初二上册课程讲义
课程编号：junior_english_g8s1　适用学段：初中　年级：初二　学期：上学期

## 第一章 比较级与最高级

### 1.1 形容词比较级和最高级的构成

- **规则变化**：一般直接加 `-er/-est`（tall→taller→tallest）；以 e 结尾加 `-r/-st`（nice→nicer→nicest）；以"辅音字母 + y"结尾，变 y 为 i 加 `-er/-est`（happy→happier→happiest）；重读闭音节双写末尾辅音字母再加 `-er/-est`（big→bigger→biggest, thin→thinner）。
- 多音节词和部分双音节词在前面加 `more/most`：`beautiful→more beautiful→most beautiful, important→more important`。
- **不规则变化**需单独记忆：`good/well→better→best, bad/badly→worse→worst, many/much→more→most, little→less→least, far→farther/further→farthest/furthest`。
- 双音节词中 `clever, quiet, narrow, simple` 等既可用 `-er/-est` 也可用 `more/most`。
- 记忆口诀："比较级加 er，最高级加 est，多音节前加 more/most，特殊变化要牢记。"

### 1.2 as...as 结构与比较句型

- **同级比较**用 `as + 形容词/副词原级 + as`（和……一样），否定用 `not as/so + 原级 + as`（不如……）。例如：`Tom is as tall as Jack.` `This book is not as interesting as that one.`
- **比较级**用 `形容词/副词比较级 + than`（比……更）。例如：`My brother is two years older than me.` `She sings better than I (do).`
- **最高级**用 `the + 形容词最高级 + of/in + 范围`，副词最高级前 the 可省略。例如：`He is the tallest student in our class.` `Of all the subjects, I like English (the) most.`
- 比较的对象要同类可比：`The weather in Beijing is colder than that in Guangzhou.`（用 that 代替 the weather，避免重复）。
- 最高级前有物主代词或所有格时不加 the：`my best friend, Tom's fastest bike`。

### 1.3 比较级常用句型

- **"越来越……"**：`比较级 and 比较级`，如 `The weather is getting colder and colder.` `Our country is becoming more and more beautiful.`
- **"越……就越……"**：`the + 比较级, the + 比较级`，如 `The harder you work, the better grades you will get.`
- 比较级前可用 `much, a little, even, far, a lot` 等程度副词修饰，表示程度差异：`This coat is much more expensive than that one.` `It's a little colder today.`
- 比较级前一般不用 very、quite、too 修饰，但可用 `much/far/a lot`（得多）与 `a little/a bit`（一点）。
- 用比较级表达最高级含义：`He is taller than any other student in his class.` = `He is the tallest student in his class.`

### 例题

**例题 1**：用所给形容词的适当形式填空。

1. This film is ___ (interesting) than that one.
2. Lily sings ___ (well) in our class.
3. My bag is not as ___ (heavy) as yours.

**参考答案**：1. more interesting；2. (the) best；3. heavy。

**例题 2**：翻译句子。

"你越努力，得到的就越多。"

**参考答案**：The more you work hard, the more you will get. / The harder you work, the more you'll get.

## 本章小结与常见错误

1. **比较级/最高级形式变化错误**：多音节词误加 `-er`（如 `more` 与 `-er` 同时使用：`more taller`），单音节词误加 `more`（`more big`），须根据音节数选择正确形式。
2. **比较对象不一致**：如误写 `The weather in Beijing is colder than Guangzhou.` 比较的对象应是天气与天气，需用 that 替代：`...colder than that in Guangzhou.`
3. **as...as 中间误用比较级**：同级比较中间必须是原级，如误写 `as taller as`，应为 `as tall as`；否定形式 `not as/so + 原级 + as`。
4. **最高级漏掉 the 或范围词**：形容词最高级前一般要加 the，范围用 of/in 引出，如误写 `He is tallest in class.` 应为 `He is the tallest in his class.`

## 第二章 一般将来时

### 2.1 will与be going to结构

- **will + 动词原形**：表示将来的动作或状态，多指临时决定、意愿或对未来的预测。`will` 用于所有人称，否定为 `won't`，一般疑问句把 will 提到句首。
- 例句：`I will call you later.` `It will rain tomorrow, I think.` `— Will you come to my party? — Yes, I will. / No, I won't.`
- **be going to + 动词原形**：表示事先计划、打算做某事，或根据现有迹象推测即将发生的事。结构中的 be 随主语变化。
- 例句：`We are going to have a picnic this Sunday.` `Look at the clouds. It's going to rain.`
- 两种结构的区别：`will` 多表临时决定，`be going to` 多表事先安排；表示将来必然发生或意愿时两者常可互换。
- `there be` 结构的将来时：`There will be a football match tomorrow.` / `There is going to be a football match.`（be going to 句型中不可写 `There is going to have`）。

### 2.2 将来时的时间状语

- 常用的将来时间状语：`tomorrow, the day after tomorrow, next week/month/year, in two days`（两天后）, `soon, later`。
- `in + 时间段` 表示"……之后"，用于一般将来时：`She will come back in a week.`（她一周后回来）
- `next` 与星期、月份连用表将来：`next Monday, next summer`；注意 `next morning` 一般不用，而说 `tomorrow morning`。
- 对将来时间提问用 `When` 或 `How soon`：`How soon will you leave? — In three days.`
- 在时间状语从句、条件状语从句中，将来时用一般现在时代替（主将从现），主句才用将来时：`I will tell him when he comes back.`

### 2.3 计划与打算表达

- 询问计划常用：`What are you going to do?`、`What will you do this weekend?`、`Do you have any plans?`
- 表达打算的常见句型：`I'm going to ...`、`I will ...`、`I plan to ...`、`I'd like to ...`、`I want to ...`
- 例：`I'm going to learn to play the guitar.` `She plans to visit her grandparents during the holiday.`
- 表示"打算成为……"（职业理想）用 `be going to be`：`He is going to be a doctor when he grows up.`
- 本册单元话题围绕"假期计划、未来生活"展开，写计划类短文时应将活动、时间、顺序交代清楚，并正确使用将来时态。

### 例题

**例题 1**：用 will 或 be going to 的适当形式填空。

1. — Look at the dark clouds. — It ___ (rain).
2. — I don't have any money. — Don't worry. I ___ (lend) you some.
3. They ___ (visit) the Science Museum next Friday.

**参考答案**：1. is going to rain（有迹象）；2. will lend（临时决定）；3. are going to visit / will visit。

**例题 2**：汉译英。

"明天我们班要开一场英语晚会。"

**参考答案**：Our class is going to have an English party tomorrow. / Our class will have an English party tomorrow.

## 本章小结与常见错误

1. **will 后接动词原形**：如误写 `I will going to school.`、`She will plays.`，will 之后必须跟动词原形；`be going to` 中 to 后也是动词原形。
2. **there be 的将来时错误**：如误写 `There is going to have a meeting.`，there be 句型表"存在"用 be 不用 have，应为 `There is going to be a meeting.`
3. **时间状语与将来时搭配不当**：如误写 `I will go to Beijing last week.`（last week 是过去时间），将来时需搭配 tomorrow、next week、in two days 等。
4. **in 的时间含义混淆**：`in two days` 在将来时中表示"两天后"，不是"两天内"；`after` 用于过去时间（after two days）或时间点后（after 5 o'clock）。

## 第三章 动词不定式

### 3.1 不定式作宾语

- **动词不定式**的基本形式为 `to + 动词原形`，在句中可作宾语、状语、定语等成分。
- 常见后接不定式作宾语的动词有：`want, hope, wish, decide, agree, learn, plan, would like, begin, start, try, forget, remember, like, love, prefer` 等。
- 例句：`I want to buy a new bike.` `She decided to study abroad.` `He hopes to visit the Great Wall.`
- `find/think/feel + it + 形容词 + to do` 结构中，it 是形式宾语，真正的宾语是后面的不定式：`I find it easy to learn English.`
- 部分动词后接不定式与动名词含义不同：`remember to do`（记得要做）、`remember doing`（记得做过）；`stop to do`（停下来去做）、`stop doing`（停止做）。

### 3.2 不定式作目的状语

- 不定式在句中作**目的状语**，回答"为什么"的问题，可译为"为了……"。
- 例句：`He came to see me.`（他来看我）、`She gets up early to catch the early bus.`
- 目的状语可置于句首加强语气：`To keep healthy, you should exercise every day.`
- 强调目的可用 `in order to` 或 `so as to`：`He studies hard in order to pass the exam.`
- 目的状语为否定形式时用 `in order not to`：`She spoke slowly in order not to be misunderstood.`

### 3.3 常见动词搭配

- **动词 + 不定式**：`want to do, decide to do, plan to do, agree to do, hope to do, learn to do, would like to do`。
- **动词 + 宾语 + 不定式**（宾补）：`ask sb. to do, tell sb. to do, want sb. to do, teach sb. to do, invite sb. to do`。否定形式为 `ask sb. not to do`。
- **动词 + 不带 to 的不定式**（感官动词和使役动词）：`see, watch, hear, feel, notice + sb. + do`，以及 `make, let, have + sb. + do`；变被动语态时须还原 to：`He was made to do the work.`
- 不定式的否定形式是在 to 前加 not：`Tell him not to be late.`（不可写成 `don't to be`）。
- 疑问词 + 不定式（wh- + to do）作宾语：`I don't know what to do.` `Can you tell me how to get to the station?`

### 例题

**例题 1**：用括号内动词的适当形式填空。

1. She decided ___ (join) the English club.
2. The teacher asked us ___ (not talk) in class.
3. He got up early ___ (catch) the first train.

**参考答案**：1. to join；2. not to talk；3. to catch。

**例题 2**：单项选择。

Let's ___ (to play / play) basketball after school.

**参考答案**：play（let sb. do，不带 to）。

## 本章小结与常见错误

1. **漏掉或误加 to**：如误写 `I want buy a pen.`（漏 to）或 `Let's to go home.`（let 后不带 to），须分清动词后接 to do 还是 do。
2. **否定形式错误**：不定式的否定是在 to 前加 not，如误写 `The teacher told us don't talk.` 应为 `The teacher told us not to talk.`
3. **宾语补足语与 to do 混搭**：如误写 `He asked me helping him.`，ask sb. 后接不定式 `to help`；感官动词、使役动词后接原形 `make sb. do`。
4. **make sb. do 的被动语态错误**：主动语态省略 to，被动语态要还原 to，如误写 `He was made do it.` 应为 `He was made to do it.`
